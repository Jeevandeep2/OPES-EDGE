10 Ways to Boost Efficiency

Simple, proven methods to cut your energy use without sacrificing comfort.

1
Switch to 5-Star Appliances

BEE 5-star rated ACs, fridges, and fans can save 20-30% electricity compared to 3-star models.

2
Use Smart Power Strips

Cut phantom loads from TVs, chargers, and computers that consume power even when "off".

3
Insulate Your Home

Proper insulation reduces heating and cooling needs by up to 40%. Seal windows and doors.

4
Clean AC Filters Monthly

Dirty filters force ACs to work harder. Cleaning them improves airflow and saves 5-15% energy.

5
Use Natural Ventilation

Open windows during cool hours. Use exhaust fans to remove hot air before turning on AC.

6
Install Motion Sensors

Automatic lights in corridors, bathrooms, and outdoor areas ensure lights are never left on.

7
Cook with Lids On

Covering pots reduces cooking time by 30%. Use pressure cookers and induction for efficiency.

8
Defrost Regularly

Frost buildup in freezers increases energy use. Defrost when ice exceeds 6mm thickness.

9
Use Task Lighting

Table lamps and under-cabinet lights use less energy than full room lighting for focused work.

10
Maintain Your Inverter Battery

Keep battery terminals clean and water levels checked. A healthy battery charges faster and wastes less.

BEE Ratings

Understanding Star Ratings

The Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) star rating helps you choose appliances that use less electricity for the same output.

5-Star = Most Efficient

Highest upfront cost but lowest running cost. Best for heavy daily use.

3-Star = Balanced

Moderate efficiency. Good for occasional use or budget constraints.

1-Star = Least Efficient

Cheapest to buy but consumes the most power. Avoid for daily-use appliances.
