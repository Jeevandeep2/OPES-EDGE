Conservation in Daily Life

Every unit saved is a unit generated. Build these habits today.

Set AC to 24°C

Every 1°C lower = 6% more power. 24°C is the sweet spot for comfort and savings.

Switch Off

Lights, fans, and chargers when leaving a room. It takes 2 seconds and saves 10% energy.

Full Loads Only

Run washing machines and dishwashers only with full loads. Use cold water when possible.

Iron in Batches

Ironing multiple clothes at once saves repeated heating. Use the right temperature setting.

Community

Conserve Together

Energy conservation works best when communities act together. Here is how your neighbourhood can contribute.

Common Area LEDs

Replace all society lights with LEDs. Motion sensors in parking and staircases.

Plant Shade Trees

Trees around buildings reduce indoor temperature by 2-4°C, cutting AC use.

Energy Audits

Conduct annual energy audits for apartments and schools to find waste points.
