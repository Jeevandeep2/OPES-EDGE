Understanding Your Energy Bill

Learn what consumes the most power in your home and how charges are calculated.

Air Conditioner

Biggest consumer. Uses 1.5-2.5 kW per hour. Set at 24°C and clean filters monthly.

Refrigerator

Runs 24/7. Uses 100-250W. Keep coils clean and set temperature to 3-5°C.

Water Heater

Uses 2-3 kW. Use solar heaters or set timer to heat only when needed.

Fans & Lights

Fans use 50-75W. LEDs use 7-10W. Together they run for hours — efficiency matters.
