Quick Wins

Save Energy Today

Small changes in daily habits can reduce your electricity bill by 20-30%.

Switch to LED

LEDs use 75% less energy and last 25x longer than incandescent bulbs.

Set AC to 24°C

Every 1°C lower increases energy use by 6%. 24°C is optimal for comfort and savings.

Unplug Devices

Phantom load from standby devices can account for 10% of your bill.

Use Natural Light

Open curtains during the day. It's free, healthy, and reduces electricity use.
