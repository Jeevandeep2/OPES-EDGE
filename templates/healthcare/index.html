When to Call an Ambulance Immediately

Chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, sudden confusion, severe burns, poisoning, or unconsciousness. Do not drive yourself — call 108.

Stay Healthy

Wellness & Prevention

Small habits create lasting health. Evidence-based guidance for everyday wellness.

Balanced Nutrition

Fill half your plate with vegetables. Include whole grains, lean protein, and healthy fats. Limit sugar and processed foods.

Regular Exercise

Aim for 150 minutes of moderate activity weekly. Walking, yoga, and swimming are excellent low-impact options.

Mental Wellness

Practice mindfulness, stay connected with loved ones, and seek help when needed. Mental health is health.

Quality Sleep

Adults need 7-9 hours of sleep. Maintain a regular schedule, avoid screens before bed, and create a calm environment.

Vaccination Awareness

Stay protected. Follow the Universal Immunization Program for children. Adults should consider annual influenza vaccination and COVID-19 boosters as recommended by health authorities.
