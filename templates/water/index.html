Quick Wins

5-Minute Water Savers

Small changes that make a big difference every day.

1
Fix Leaky Taps

A single dripping tap wastes up to 20 litres per day. Fix it today and save 7,000+ litres a year.

2
Use a Bucket

A bucket bath uses 15-20 litres. A shower can use 80+. Switch and save 60+ litres per bath.

3
Full Load Only

Run washing machines and dishwashers only with full loads. Saves water, energy, and money.

4
Reuse RO Water

RO reject water is perfect for mopping, gardening, and flushing. Don't let it go down the drain.

5
Water Plants Early

Water gardens early morning or evening to reduce evaporation losses by up to 30%.

6
Install Aerators

Tap aerators mix air with water, cutting flow by 50% without reducing pressure. Costs under ₹50.
