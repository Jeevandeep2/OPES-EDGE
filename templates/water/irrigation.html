Why Drip Irrigation Wins

Drip irrigation delivers water directly to the plant root zone, drop by drop. It is the most efficient method for most crops.

Water Directly to Roots

No water wasted on weeds or between rows. Every drop reaches the plant.

Reduces Disease

Dry foliage means fewer fungal diseases. Plants stay healthier with less pesticide.

Higher Yields

Consistent moisture improves crop quality and can increase yields by 20-50%.

Government Subsidy

PMKSY provides 55-75% subsidy on drip irrigation systems for small farmers.

Soil Moisture Awareness

Use the simple finger test before watering. Most plants are overwatered, not underwatered.

Dry Soil

Soil feels dry 5cm below surface. Crumbly texture. Plant leaves may droop slightly.

Water Now
Moist Soil

Soil feels cool and slightly damp 5cm down. Holds shape when squeezed. Ideal condition.

Perfect — Wait
Wet Soil

Soil is muddy or water pools on surface. Squeezed soil drips water. Risk of root rot.

Smart Scheduling

When and how much to water for maximum efficiency.

1
Check Weather First

Skip watering if rain is forecast within 24 hours. Use weather apps or a simple rain gauge.

2
Deep, Less Often

Water deeply 2-3 times a week instead of light daily watering. Encourages deeper roots.

3
Morning is Best

Water between 5-8 AM. Cooler temperatures reduce evaporation by 30-50%.

4
Avoid Evening on Foliage

Wet leaves overnight promote fungal diseases. If evening watering is needed, target roots only.

5
Group by Water Needs

Plant water-loving crops together and drought-resistant crops separately. Zone your irrigation.

6
Use Mulch Everywhere

5cm of organic mulch reduces evaporation by 70% and keeps soil temperature stable.
