Safety First

Never drink or cook with reused water unless it has been treated to potable standards by a certified system. This page covers non-potable reuse only. When in doubt, consult a water quality professional.

Greywater

Reusing Greywater

Water from sinks, showers, and washing machines can have a second life.

What is Greywater?

Greywater is gently used water from bathroom sinks, showers, tubs, and washing machines. It does not include water from toilets (blackwater) or kitchen sinks with food waste.

Safe for: Garden irrigation, toilet flushing, floor cleaning, car washing (with biodegradable soap)
Simple Greywater Rules
  • Use within 24 hours to prevent bacterial growth
  • Never store greywater without treatment
  • Avoid water with bleach, strong detergents, or fabric softeners
  • Do not use on root vegetables or edible leaves
  • Apply directly to soil, not on plant foliage
Applications

Practical Reuse Ideas

Match the water source to the right reuse application.

Garden Irrigation

RO reject water, AC condensate, and cooled cooking water are perfect for gardens. Nutrients in greywater can even fertilize plants.

Low Effort
Toilet Flushing

Toilets use 30-40% of household water. A simple bucket system or dual plumbing can redirect greywater to flush tanks.

High Impact
Floor & Car Cleaning

Water used for rinsing clothes or washing vegetables can be collected for mopping floors and washing vehicles.

Treatment

Treatment Required

Different uses need different levels of treatment.

No Treatment Needed
  • RO reject water for gardening
  • AC condensate for plants
  • Cooled cooking water for gardens
  • Rainwater for gardening (after basic filtration)
Basic Filtration
  • Greywater for garden (sand filter + settling)
  • Washing machine water for toilet flushing
  • Shower water for floor cleaning
  • Requires: mesh filter + 24-hour use rule
Advanced Treatment
  • Any reuse for human contact (play areas)
  • Reuse in food processing
  • Requires: biological + disinfection treatment
  • Must meet local reuse standards
