The Journey

From Sky to Supply

How rainwater travels from clouds to your tap.

Rain

Monsoon clouds deliver pure, soft water free of salts and chemicals.

Collection

Rooftops, courtyards, and paved areas channel water into pipes.

Filtration

First-flush diverters and sand filters remove leaves, dust, and debris.

Storage

Underground sumps, overhead tanks, or recharge pits hold the water.

Reuse / Recharge

Use for gardening, flushing, or let it recharge groundwater aquifers.

Systems

Harvesting Systems

Choose the right setup for your home or community.

Rooftop Collection

The most common method. Gutters collect rain from roofs and direct it to storage tanks or recharge pits.

  • Best for: Homes, schools, offices
  • Cost: ₹5,000 – ₹25,000
  • Capacity: 100-1,000+ litres per rainfall
Surface Runoff

Captures water from courtyards, driveways, and open ground. Ideal for large plots and farms.

  • Best for: Farms, large campuses
  • Cost: ₹10,000 – ₹50,000
  • Benefit: Reduces flooding and soil erosion
Recharge Pits

Underground pits filled with gravel and sand filter rainwater directly into the groundwater table.

  • Best for: Areas with falling water tables
  • Cost: ₹15,000 – ₹40,000
  • Benefit: Raises groundwater levels

Care

System Maintenance

Keep your harvesting system efficient year-round.

1
Clean Gutters

Remove leaves and debris before every monsoon. Clogged gutters overflow and waste precious rain.

2
First-Flush Diverter

Always install a first-flush diverter. The first 5-10 minutes of rain carries the most dust and contaminants.

3
Check Filters

Clean sand and mesh filters every 2-3 months. Replace filter media annually for best performance.

4
Tank Cleaning

Drain and scrub storage tanks once a year before the monsoon. Prevent algae and mosquito breeding.
