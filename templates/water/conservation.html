Water Conservation

Practical, actionable ways to reduce water waste at home, on the farm, and in your community.

At Home

Home Water Savers

Simple changes that cut household water use by 30-50%.

1
Fix Leaks Immediately

A dripping tap wastes 20+ litres/day. A running toilet wastes 200+ litres/day. Check under sinks and behind toilets monthly.

Tip: Add food colouring to the toilet tank. If colour appears in the bowl without flushing, you have a leak.
2
Install Low-Flow Fixtures

Low-flow showerheads use 6-8 L/min vs 15-20 L/min for regular ones. Aerators on taps cut flow by 50% without losing pressure.

Cost: Aerators cost ₹30-80. Showerheads ₹200-500. Payback in 1-2 months.
3
Reuse Greywater

Water from washing machines, sinks, and baths can water plants, flush toilets, and clean floors. Never use for drinking or cooking.

Note: Use biodegradable soaps if reusing for gardening.
4
Smart Appliances

Always run washing machines and dishwashers with full loads. A half-load uses nearly as much water as a full load.

Save: Full loads can save 15,000+ litres per year for a family of four.
5
RO Reject Water

RO purifiers reject 2-3 litres for every 1 litre purified. Collect this water in a bucket for mopping, gardening, or flushing.

Save: A typical home RO can recover 15-25 litres/day of reject water.
6
Turn Off While Soaping

Turn off the tap while brushing teeth, soaping hands, or shampooing. This simple habit saves 10-15 litres per person daily.

Impact: A family of 4 can save 20,000+ litres/year with this alone.
Outdoors

Garden & Outdoor Tips

Keep your garden green without wasting water.

Smart Outdoor Watering
Water Early or Late

Water between 5-8 AM or 6-9 PM to reduce evaporation by up to 30%. Midday watering loses 50%+ to evaporation.

Mulch Your Soil

A 5cm layer of mulch (dry leaves, straw, or wood chips) reduces evaporation by 70% and suppresses weeds.

Drip Over Spray

Drip irrigation delivers water directly to roots with 90%+ efficiency. Sprinklers lose 30-50% to wind and evaporation.

Choose Native Plants

Native and drought-resistant plants need 50-80% less water than exotic lawns and gardens.

Together

Community Conservation

When neighbours act together, the impact multiplies.

Apartment Complex Audits

Conduct a water audit in your apartment complex. Check for leaks in common pipes, optimize pump timings, and install dual-flush toilets in all units.

Potential savings: 20-40% reduction in common area water bills.

Community Rain Gardens

Create rain gardens in common areas to capture runoff, recharge groundwater, and reduce flooding. Use native plants that tolerate both wet and dry conditions.

Benefit: Recharges groundwater and reduces stormwater runoff by 30%.

Awareness Drives

Organize monthly water awareness sessions in your community. Share bills publicly, celebrate the lowest-use household, and teach children about water conservation.

Impact: Communities with awareness programs reduce use by 15-25%.

Shared Maintenance

Form a volunteer team to check community taps, pipes, and tanks monthly. Early leak detection prevents massive water loss and property damage.

Tip: Use a simple logbook to track readings and spot anomalies early.
