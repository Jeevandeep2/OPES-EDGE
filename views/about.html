About OPES EDGE

One platform for everyday community progress.

OPES EDGE connects agriculture, education, healthcare, community services, energy, and water in one student-built platform.

The project is designed to grow carefully: useful local workflows first, then integration-ready support for live services, sensors, and AI when the required systems are available.
